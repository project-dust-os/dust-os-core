# Privacy Policy

Status: **internal policy**, 22 November 2026. This document describes how the
DUST-OS project handles personal data. It is not legal advice and has not been
reviewed by counsel.

The project is private and pre-alpha. It operates no public service and collects
no telemetry from the operating system.

---

- **Owner:** devennw
- **Contact:** dvennote65@gmail.com

## 2. Scope

This policy covers personal data processed in:

- the private source repository and issue tracker;
- contracts, NDAs, and IP assignments with team members and partners;
- customer and prospective-customer communication;
- support requests;
- security reports;
- any future website or customer portal.

It does not cover third-party services that have their own privacy policies.

## 3. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and contact | Name, handle, email, organisation, country | You, your employer, or a contract |
| Contract data | Signed agreements, role, access level, start and end dates | You and the Owner |
| Contributor data | Git author name and email, commits, review comments | Git and the issue tracker |
| Support data | Messages, logs, serial output, hardware details | You |
| Security reports | Reporter contact, vulnerability details, evidence | You |
| Billing data | Invoice details, tax identifiers | You or your employer |
| Technical data | Authentication logs, access logs, IP address | Repository and hosting providers |

We do not intentionally collect special-category data (health, religion,
biometrics, political opinions). Do not send it.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

Under Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection, processing is
based on one or more of the following:

| Purpose | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Performing a contract or engagement | Contract |
| Managing access and security | Legitimate interest; legal obligation |
| Responding to support and security reports | Consent or legitimate interest |
| Keeping tax, accounting, and contract records | Legal obligation |
| Protecting legal rights and resolving disputes | Legitimate interest |
| Sending project updates, if any | Consent, with opt-out |

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. The operating system

- DUST-OS does not include telemetry, analytics, or a unique device identifier by
  default.
- If a future product includes diagnostics, it will be opt-in, described in the
  product documentation, and disabled unless the customer enables it.
- Crash dumps may contain memory contents. Treat them as Confidential, minimise
  collection, and delete them when the issue is closed.
- Customer data must never be used in development or testing without a written
  basis and anonymisation.

## 6. Sharing

Personal data is shared only with:

- service providers that host the repository, email, or accounting systems,
  under appropriate confidentiality and data-processing terms;
- professional advisers, such as a lawyer or accountant, under confidentiality;
- authorities, when required by law or to protect legal rights;
- a successor entity, in a genuine reorganisation or transfer, with notice.

We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 7. International transfers

Some providers may store data outside Indonesia. Before transferring personal
data internationally, the project will use a lawful transfer mechanism and apply
appropriate safeguards, as required by applicable law.

## 8. Retention

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Contract and IP records | Duration of the engagement plus 10 years, or as required by law |
| Tax and accounting records | As required by Indonesian law |
| Support tickets | 2 years after closure |
| Security reports | 3 years after closure, or longer if a legal claim is possible |
| Access logs | 12 months, unless needed for an incident |
| Contributor git metadata | As long as the repository history exists |
| Marketing consent records | Until consent is withdrawn, plus 2 years |

When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

## 9. Security

- Access is role-based and requires multi-factor authentication.
- Restricted data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Secrets are stored in an approved secret store, never in the repository.
- Access is reviewed every six months and removed when an engagement ends.
- Suspected personal-data breaches are handled under
  [DOCUMENTS/IP_POLICY.md](DOCUMENTS/IP_POLICY.md), Section 12, and reported to
  the authorities and affected individuals where required.

No system is perfectly secure. Report a concern to the contact above.

## 10. Minors

The project owner is a minor. Team members and contributors under 18 must have a
parent or guardian consent to the processing of their personal data and to the
relevant agreements. Data about minors is kept to the minimum necessary.

## 11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

- access to your personal data;
- correction of inaccurate data;
- deletion;
- restriction of processing;
- portability, where applicable;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdrawal of consent, without affecting prior lawful processing;
- a complaint to the competent data-protection authority.

Requests are answered within the period required by law. Identity may be verified
before a request is fulfilled.

## 12. Cookies and websites

If a project website is launched, it will use the minimum cookies necessary and
will describe them in a cookie notice. Analytics will be opt-in or absent.

## 13. Changes

This policy may be updated. A material change will be announced to team members
and, where required, to affected individuals.
