## Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made on `[DATE]` between:

**Party A:** `[FULL LEGAL NAME OF OWNER OR ENTITY]`, `[FORM]`, at `[ADDRESS]`, represented by `[NAME, ROLE]`; and

**Party B:** `[FULL LEGAL NAME]`, `[FORM]`, at `[ADDRESS]`, represented by `[NAME, ROLE]`.

Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties". The Agreement is mutual unless a
Schedule says otherwise.

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to discuss `[PURPOSE: evaluation, partnership, hardware port, investment, audit, other]` (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to each other for that Purpose.

### 2. Confidential information

2.1 **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient"), in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood as confidential, including:

    (a) source code, object code, firmware, designs, architecture, and technical data;

    (b) security properties, vulnerabilities, and incident information;

    (c) roadmaps, pricing, customer and supplier lists, and business plans;

    (d) samples, prototypes, and evaluation hardware;

    (e) the existence and content of the discussions.

2.2 Confidential Information includes notes, summaries, and analyses derived from it.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

    (a) is or becomes public without breach of this Agreement;

    (b) the Recipient already lawfully held without a confidentiality obligation;

    (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality obligation;

    (d) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or

    (e) the Discloser approves in writing for release.

### 4. Obligations

The Recipient must:

    (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

    (b) protect it with at least reasonable care, and no less than it uses for its own confidential information;

    (c) disclose it only to employees, contractors, and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality at least as strict as this Agreement;

    (d) not copy it except as necessary for the Purpose;

    (e) not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or hardware provided, except as permitted by mandatory law;

    (f) not export or transfer it in breach of export-control law; and

    (g) notify the Discloser promptly of any suspected loss or unauthorised disclosure.

### 5. Compelled disclosure

If law, regulation, or a court requires disclosure, the Recipient notifies the Discloser promptly where lawful, discloses only the required portion, and cooperates to seek confidential treatment.

### 6. Term

6.1 This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues for `[NUMBER]` years for the Purpose.

6.2 Confidentiality obligations continue for `[NUMBER]` years after disclosure, or indefinitely for source code, trade secrets, and security information.

### 7. Return and destruction

On written request or at the end of the Purpose, the Recipient returns or destroys all Confidential Information, including copies and derived material, and certifies destruction in writing within `[NUMBER]` days. One archival copy may be retained by legal counsel solely to prove compliance, subject to continuing confidentiality.

### 8. No licence or obligation

8.1 No licence or ownership right is granted, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

8.2 No Party is obliged to disclose anything, enter a further agreement, or proceed with a transaction.

8.3 Each Party bears its own costs.

### 9. No warranty

Confidential Information is provided "AS IS", without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose. The Discloser is not liable for reliance on it.

### 10. Remedies

10.1 The Parties agree that breach may cause harm for which damages are inadequate.

10.2 The Discloser may seek injunctive relief and specific performance, in addition to other remedies, without proving actual loss or posting a bond where the court permits.

10.3 A Recipient in breach reimburses reasonable legal costs.

### 11. Export control and sanctions

11.1 Technical data, especially cryptographic code, may be subject to export-control law.

11.2 The Recipient must not export, re-export, or transfer Confidential Information to a restricted destination, person, or entity, and must comply with the laws of the Republic of Indonesia and other applicable jurisdictions.

### 12. Governing law and disputes

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.

12.2 The Parties attempt amicable resolution for `[NUMBER]` days. Failing that, the dispute is submitted to `[COURT / ARBITRATION FORUM]` in `[CITY]`, in `[LANGUAGE]`.

12.3 A Party may seek urgent injunctive relief in a competent court.

### 13. General

13.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement on confidentiality and supersedes prior understandings on that subject.

13.2 A variation is effective only in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a genuine reorganisation.

13.4 If a provision is unenforceable, it is modified to the minimum extent necessary; the rest remains in effect.

13.5 No waiver is effective unless in writing.

13.6 The English text controls if this Agreement is translated.

13.7 Notices are given in writing to the addresses above.
