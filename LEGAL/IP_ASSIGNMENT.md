## Intellectual Property Assignment and Confidentiality Agreement

This Agreement is made on `[DATE]` between:

purposes of consent and enforceability.

### 1. Background

1.1 The Assignor provides services to the DUST-OS project as `[ROLE: employee / contractor / intern / co-founder / other]` from `[START DATE]`.

1.2 The Parties intend that all intellectual property created in connection with that role belongs to the Assignee.

1.3 This Agreement is separate from, and additional to, any services or employment agreement. If there is a conflict on ownership, this Agreement controls.

### 2. Definitions

2.1 **"Work"** means all code, firmware, drivers, documentation, designs, specifications, tests, artwork, data, and other material created, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Assignor, alone or with others, in connection with the project, during the engagement and for `[NUMBER]` months after it, including work created before the effective date that is incorporated into the project.

2.2 **"IP Rights"** means all intellectual property rights worldwide, including copyright, database rights, patents, trade secrets, mask works, design rights, and rights in know-how, whether registered or not, and all applications, renewals, and extensions.

2.3 **"Moral Rights"** means rights of attribution, integrity, and similar rights that cannot be transferred under applicable law, including under Law No. 28 of 2014 on Copyright of the Republic of Indonesia.

### 3. Assignment

3.1 The Assignor **assigns** to the Assignee, with full title guarantee, all right, title, and interest in and to the Work and the IP Rights, effective on creation.

3.2 The assignment is present and automatic. No further document is required for each item of Work, but the Assignor will sign one on request.

3.3 The Assignor assigns all rights in future Work created during the engagement.

3.4 To the extent any Work is created before this Agreement, the Assignor assigns it on signature.

3.5 Where the law treats the Work as a work made for hire, the Assignee is the author and owner from creation. This clause is additional to Section 3.1, not a substitute.

### 4. Fallback licence

4.1 If an assignment is held invalid or ineffective for any Work, the Assignor grants the Assignee an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable, and sublicensable licence to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, distribute, sublicense, and otherwise exploit the Work and the IP Rights.

4.2 The fallback licence includes the right to bring and defend claims for past, present, and future infringement.

### 5. Moral rights

5.1 To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Assignor consents to, and waives, any Moral Rights that would prevent the Assignee or its licensees from using, modifying, combining, licensing, or distributing the Work, including the right to decide whether to attribute authorship and how.

5.2 Where waiver is not permitted, the Assignor gives an irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free consent to those acts and agrees not to assert Moral Rights against the Assignee, its licensees, or customers.

5.3 The Assignor retains the right to be identified as the author where required by mandatory law. Attribution practice is described in `AUTHORS.md`.

### 6. Further assurances

6.1 The Assignor will, at the Assignee's expense, sign documents and take reasonable steps to perfect, record, register, maintain, and enforce the IP Rights.

6.2 If the Assignor cannot be found or refuses without good reason, the Assignor irrevocably appoints the Assignee as attorney-in-fact for those limited purposes.

### 7. Representations and warranties

The Assignor represents and warrants that:

    (a) the Work is original, or the Assignor has all rights needed to assign it;

    (b) the Work does not infringe a third-party right;

    (c) the Work contains no GPL, LGPL, AGPL, or SSPL code, and no code of unknown provenance;

    (d) all third-party code, data, and assets are disclosed in writing and approved by the Assignee;

    (e) the Work is not subject to a prior assignment, licence, or obligation that conflicts with this Agreement;

    (f) the Assignor has disclosed any known defect, limitation, or security issue;

    (g) the Assignor has the legal capacity to enter this Agreement, or the Guardian has consented under Section 12.

### 8. Confidentiality

8.1 The Assignor keeps all non-public project information confidential, uses it only to perform the engagement, and does not disclose it.

8.2 This obligation continues after the engagement ends, for as long as the information remains confidential, and is additional to any NDA.

8.3 The Assignor returns or destroys confidential material on request and certifies destruction in writing.

### 9. Consideration

9.1 The Assignor acknowledges receipt of `[SALARY / CONTRACT FEE / IDR 1 AND OTHER GOOD AND VALUABLE CONSIDERATION]`, which is adequate consideration for this Agreement.

9.2 The Assignor has no right to additional payment for the assignment, except as stated in a separate written agreement.

### 10. Term and termination

10.1 This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues until all Work and IP Rights are assigned and all confidentiality obligations end.

10.2 Termination of the engagement does not terminate this Agreement or revoke any assignment.

10.3 Sections 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11, and 12 survive.

### 11. Governing law and disputes

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.

11.2 The Parties attempt amicable resolution for `[NUMBER]` days. Failing that, the dispute is submitted to `[COURT / ARBITRATION FORUM]` in `[CITY]`.
